GAMEPLAY MECHANICS & FEATURES

Cold-Gas Thruster Jumps

Draft in the slipstream of 18-wheeler trucks to charge aerodynamic lift, then fire downward cold-gas plasma thrusters to execute a clean 360° aerial stunt roll!

Sculpted 3D Hypercar

Streamlined unibody chassis with 16-segment turbine radial wheels, active aerodynamics, steering camber roll, and customizable cyber liveries.

3D Highway Pileup Physics

Colliding traffic realistically tumbles, barrel-rolls, skids across asphalt with friction sparks, and generates billowing debris smoke plumes.

Persistent score tracking with RESTful JSON API. Fully prepared and configured for 1-click cloud deployment on Render, Railway, Fly.io, or Heroku.
